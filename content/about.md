A World in Alef begins with **ا**, alef: a small written shape that opens the door to words, stories and everyday conversations.

This is a welcoming space for adults learning Iranian Persian. The lessons start with listening and pictures, move into the alphabet, and gradually build toward language you can use.

## What you’ll find here

- A clear beginner pathway, with one small goal at a time.
- Practice materials that make room for repetition and mistakes.
- Notes on language and culture, with space for the journey to grow.

The name leaves room to explore beyond the classroom, too: books, places, daily life and the questions that arrive along the way.
