---
title: "A two-minute practice you can actually repeat"
description: "Look, cover, recall, check. Then try again tomorrow."
date: 2026-09-28
---

A long study session is not the only way to practise. Here is a small routine to try after a lesson.

## Choose two familiar words

Use words that have already been introduced. Say each one with its model and connect it to a picture or a meaning.

## Cover the answer

Look away from the written model. Can you say the word? If you are practising reading, reveal the Persian spelling but keep the translation covered.

## Check, without turning it into a verdict

A missed word tells you what needs another encounter. Correct it, try the other word, and then return to the first.

## Leave a cue for tomorrow

Put the two cards somewhere you will see them. Before opening your notes next time, give recall another try.

This is a practice suggestion, not a promise about how fast you will learn. Adjust it to your time, energy and goals.
