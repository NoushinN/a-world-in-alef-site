A new language can feel like a whole page of things you do not know. Let’s begin with one thing you can.

In this space, the first wins will be small on purpose: recognizing a spoken word, finding the beginning of a line, noticing a dot that changes a letter.

The beginner pathway will grow lesson by lesson. Beside the videos, you’ll find practice pages and picture cards to return to in your own time.

And there is room to wander. Language leads to stories, places, culture and everyday life. That is the “world” in A World in Alef.
