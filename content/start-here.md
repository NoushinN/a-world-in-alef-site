## Start with a sound, not a test

You do not need to memorize a whole alphabet before enjoying your first word. Begin with listening and meaning, then notice how the written shapes fit together.

## Your first three steps

1. **Meet lesson one.** Start with reading direction and three everyday spoken words.
2. **Try the practice.** When the download is available, use it after the video. Attempt the questions before opening the key.
3. **Come back tomorrow.** See what you can remember before looking at your notes. One remembered word is progress.

## Choose the path that fits you

**Completely new to Persian?** Follow the lessons in order. New sounds and spellings are introduced gradually.

**You speak some Persian but do not read it yet?** Begin with the alphabet lessons and focus on shapes, joins and written vowels.

**Here for culture and a little curiosity?** Visit the journal. You can start with a note, a word or a question.
